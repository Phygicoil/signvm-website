As intelligence acts on our behalf, giving our word becomes more consequential.

Signet makes that act personal, physical and verifiable.

The gesture

A pause before you seal.

Review the words, work or request on your phone. Touch the ring to sign. Your signature is bound to exactly what you approved. Change the content, and the signature no longer matches.

Explore functions

One instrument, many applications. What the ring signs depends on the software that asks, and this list grows as more apps and agents add Signet.

Set an AI mandate

Give your AI signed standing instructions. Create a verifiable record of its task, the authority you have given it and the limits you have set.

Control a LUKSO agent

Choose whether an agent can send tokens, transfer digital assets, update your profile or use approved apps through your Universal Profile. Grant, change or revoke its permissions with your ring.

Delegate to other people

Give someone signed instructions they can verify came from you before carrying them out.

Sign your work

Sign a text, image or document, whether you created it or your AI did. Others can verify your signature and check that the content is unchanged.

Mint a moment

Touch your ring to a phone to mint an autograph, dedication or shared moment as an NFT, signed by you.

Unlock access

Use your ring as a programmable key to events, connected digital spaces or compatible physical entrances. Permissions can be granted, updated or revoked.

Gate AI actionsIn development

Require your ring’s approval before a connected system can carry out a protected action. Without your signature, that action cannot proceed.

Approve Ethereum transactionsIn development

Use your ring as a signer on a Safe multisig wallet. Your signature counts towards the approvals required to carry out a transaction.

Carry your membershipIn development

Link your ring to a digital membership, with access to the benefits, services and experiences it includes. Built on the Request Seal SDK.

Build with SignetIn development

Add ring approval to your own app using the Request Seal SDK. Choose which actions require a signature before they can proceed.

The object

For five thousand years, a signet carried a person’s word. In Signet 01 the mark is the machine: the labyrinth is the antenna, tuned to the chip at its centre. No screen. No battery. Nothing to charge.

Designer

Jim Hannon-Tan developed his practice in Melbourne, Milan, London and Amsterdam, working with Alessi, Tom Dixon and Marcel Wanders. He designed Signet 01 in Adelaide.

‘The bridge between a physical object and the digital world is usually an NFC tag added to an existing design. That felt like a missed opportunity. I wondered whether the antenna could become the object itself.

‘In its spiral I recognised a labyrinth, a single path winding towards a centre. Silver is both precious and a conductor, so the form and the material could make the technology work.

‘That led me back to the signet ring, a personal seal carried on the hand. With a cryptographic key held inside, a touch could become a signature.’
