06 — Security Architecture

Security models fail when people lose the device.

People do not lose heirlooms.

Decades of research on psychological ownership confirms: objects tied to identity become extensions of the self. People resist losing them, replacing them, discarding them. Heirlooms are passed to children. Gadgets are abandoned in drawers.

88% of people hoard unused electronics. Nobody hoards a signet ring.

This is engineered retention. A chip in a plastic ring gets left on a counter. A machined spiral in 18K gold does not. The preciousness of the object is a security architecture decision, not an aesthetic one.
