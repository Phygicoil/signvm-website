The object

A signet used to be pressed into wax. The mark carried its owner. Signet 01 keeps the gesture and seals with a signature instead of wax.

The square spiral on the face is the antenna. The silver is the machine. Your phone powers the chip when you touch the ring to it. No battery.

The ring is made to be opened, so the chip can be serviced without replacing the silver.

Designed by Jim Hannon-Tan in Adelaide.

The gesture

Write a line, or choose an image or a document. Review the exact content. Touch the ring to the phone. Share the seal. A recipient can check that this ring signed this content, and that the content has not changed.

Signing on the chip is demonstrated. The application is in development. Supported phones will be confirmed before delivery.
