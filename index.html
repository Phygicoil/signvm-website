SIGNVM

Machines execute.
Humans authorise.

For thousands of years, the signet ring was the instrument of authority. Pressed into wax, it sealed a decree. Possession was proof.

In the age of artificial intelligence, that logic returns. Your agents act. Your voice can be cloned. Your face, replicated. But the ring in your hand — that is still yours.

A signet ring for the artificial age.
