The object

Emperors, popes and merchants sealed their decisions with a signet. For five thousand years it was how the world knew a decision was final, and whose it was. Signet 01 does that job for the age of AI.

Cast in sterling silver. The square spiral on the face is the antenna, twelve turns in the metal. The mark is the machine. No screen. No battery. Nothing to charge.

Twenty will be issued, numbered 01 to 20. Delivery from Q1 2027. USD 4,800.

Designed by Jim Hannon-Tan in Adelaide. Built with specialists in casting, jewellery, RF, electronics, mechanics and software.

The gesture

Your agent runs your world while you live in it. It books, buys, sends and negotiates. When it reaches a decision you have kept for yourself, it stops and asks.

The request lands on your phone in plain words. What, how much, to whom. You read it. You touch the ring to the phone. Done.

The ring signs that exact request with a key only it holds.
