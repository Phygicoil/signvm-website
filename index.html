The object

For centuries, a signet put its bearer’s mark to something that mattered. Signet 01 carries that gesture into digital life.

Cast in sterling silver. The square spiral on the face is the antenna, twelve turns in the metal. The mark is the machine. No screen. No battery. Nothing to charge.

Designed by Jim Hannon-Tan in Adelaide. Built with specialists in casting, jewellery, RF, electronics, mechanics and software.

The technology
Chip
ARX HaLo secure element. It creates the cryptographic signature.
Key
secp256k1, generated inside the chip. Cannot be exported or copied.
Connection
NFC, touched to a phone. No battery, no charging.
Phones
NFC-equipped phones. Supported models will be confirmed before delivery.
Account
A LUKSO Universal Profile can associate the ring with an account and defined permissions.
Networks
Compatible integrations can use its signatures on LUKSO, Ethereum, Base or ordinary web services.
Collectibles
Minting creates a separate on-chain record. The ring signs the authorisation.
Verification
Checks the signing key, and whether the content matches the signed record.
Limits
A signature shows that this ring signed this content. On its own it does not establish original authorship or verified legal identity.
Status
Signing on the chip is demonstrated. The Signet application is in development.
